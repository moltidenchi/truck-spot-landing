Truck Spot Style Guide

The design handoff for the logo and landing page. Instead of a Figma file, everything here is the real thing: the live components use the same CSS custom properties that are listed below, and the same values ship as tokens.json (W3C design tokens format) and tokens.css, so a designer can rebuild the library in any tool and a developer can use the values directly.

Logo

A map pin leaning into the direction of travel, with a scalloped food-truck awning in its head and three speed lines behind it: find a spot, street food, on the move. The wordmark is Big Shoulders Display Black, slanted 8° to match the pin, converted to outlines so the files need no font.

Use It Like This

  • Clear space on every side: the height of the pin head (a quarter of the icon height).
  • Minimum size: horizontal lockup 120 px wide on screen; icon 24 px. Below 24 px use the favicon, which drops the speed lines.
  • On mustard, white or fog use the colour logo; on asphalt or photos use “on dark” or mono white; on ketchup use mono white.

Please Don’t

  • Straighten the pin or the wordmark; the lean is the motion.
  • Recolour the awning or put the colour icon on ketchup red.
  • Retype the wordmark in another font or add effects and outlines.

Colour

Ketchup and mustard on asphalt and white: the colours of the street-food counter. Mustard carries big surfaces, ketchup marks the things you act on, and asphalt is the ink. Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x, computed from the tokens by the test suite.

  • Asphalt #1D2127 --color-asphalt Ink: text, dark surfaces, wordmark 16.2:1 on white, 1.0:1 on asphalt
  • Asphalt Soft #343A44 --color-asphalt-soft Hover state of dark buttons 11.4:1 on white, 1.4:1 on asphalt
  • Ketchup #C8281B --color-ketchup Brand red: logo pin, primary buttons, links on light 5.6:1 on white, 2.9:1 on asphalt

Typography

Display: Big Shoulders Display, 700–900

Lunch is parked two blocks away.

Aa Bb Cc 0123456789 & ? !

Condensed, sign-painted energy from Chicago’s city signage; headlines, truck names and the wordmark. Sentence case for headlines, Title Case for buttons and section titles.

Text: Atkinson Hyperlegible, 400 and 700

Open now until 9:30 PM. Corn tortillas pressed to order, salsas from mild to volcanic.

Il1 O0 rn m 5S 8B

Designed by the Braille Institute for maximum character distinction, so menus, prices and hours stay readable on small screens and in sunlight.
